About Us

What is PlantGuard?

PlantGuard is an IOT-based system designed to help users monitor and take care of plants using sensors and an ESP32 microcontroller.

How does the app work?

The system collects information such as soil moisture, temperature, and humidity, then sends that data through the internet to a mobile application and web dashboard.

What can users do in the app?

Users can monitor their plants remotely and receive notifications when the plant needs water. An automatic watering feature can activate a small water pump when the soil becomes too dry.
