PlantGuard is Easy to Use

  1. Place the PlantGuard device in the soil next to your plant.

    1

  2. Set the solar panel where it gets plenty of sunlight.

    2

  3. Open the PlantGuard app and tap Connect to pair your device.

    3

  4. Choose your plant so PlantGuard knows how much water it needs.

    4

  5. Check live soil moisture, humidity and temperature readings.

    5

  6. Get notified when it's time to water, or let automatic watering do it for you.

    6
